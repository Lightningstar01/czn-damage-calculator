# Magna

What the owner confirmed about this character's cards. The general rules are in [FORMULA.md](../FORMULA.md).

- **Magna** (owner's screenshots, 2026-11-08; level 60, Attack 489, Defense 384, Health 774, Justice Damage 15.0% (+16.0%), E2; Basic 5/10, Unique 1/10;
  3-1 and 7 unlocked, 5-1 locked): every in-game % is Prydwen's base x 1.10 (Basic) or x 1.02 (Unique), except where listed below.
  - Prydwen corrections: Ice Fragment and its epiphanies give 3 Crystallization (Prydwen 4; I: 5, Prydwen 6). Ice Fragment IV's damage comes at the
    end of the turn (Prydwen: start). Ice Fragment V raises the next Counterattack by 100% (Prydwen 30%). Ice Wall II gives 2 Counterattack and
    "+30% Shield Gain Amount for each Counterattack of this unit" (Prydwen: 3 Counterattack, all enemies for 1 turn). Ice Wall IV's damage is
    Fixed Damage equal to Shield held (Prydwen: "Damage"). Frost Charge III gives 4 Crystallization and 3 Vulnerable to targets hit by Counterattack
    (Prydwen: 2 Vulnerable only). Frozen Fist and Frost Shield show 3-1's 1 Counterattack. Nodes 5-1 and 7 are "or higher" (Prydwen "higher than").
    E5 names the Signature Card Upgrade (Prydwen: Unique).
  - Not raised at Unique level 1: Ice Fragment IV stays 200% (no upgrade, like Maribell's Resolute Blitz V).
  - **Counterattack**: one play per Counterattack, 150%, or 300% with "Completely blocked by Shield" on, as Maribell's (User, 2026-11-01). It joins
    the deck with the cards that give it, isn't raised by card upgrades, and isn't a card. Ice Fragment III's 30% (every Counterattack while it's in
    the deck), Ice Fragment V's 100% (switch "Ice Fragment V (next Counterattack)" on the next one) and E6's Enhanced Counterattack (30%, a switch per
    Counterattack) multiply it ("by X%", no "+"). Whether they each multiply: **Unconfirmed**, in-game test `magna-readings`.
  - **Absolute Zero** (User, 2026-11-08): "by 20% for each Counterattack on this unit" is one multiplier, x(1 + 20% x N) (30% from E3), N set per play
    as "Counterattack owned", up to 10. **Unconfirmed** in game; in-game test `magna-readings`.
  - **Glacial Iron Fist III** (User, 2026-11-08): "When Shield is owned" is a switch per play ("Holding Shield"), off until turned on.
  - **Ice Wall IV**: Fixed Damage = the play's "Shield owned", read in game (`fixedOf`, as Maribell's Shelter Strike); nothing else raises it.
    **Unconfirmed**, in-game test `magna-readings`.
  - **Node 7** (User, 2026-11-08): its Justice-Attribute Damage is in the stats screen's Justice Damage (15.0% white at Defense 384: +5% and the capped
    +10%), so nothing is added on top. Adelheid's, Rossweisse's and Tenebria's screens show their node 7 the same way.
  - Frost Charge IV's 3 hits to random enemies all hit the one target.
  - Not counted: Shields and Shield gain (Crystallization, node 5-1, DMG-Based Shield, Frost Charge I, Ice Wall II), Shield Retention, E2's Freeze
    (50% Defense-Based Damage to adjacent monsters: a fight has one target), E4 (Counterattack no longer reduces Vulnerable: set Vulnerable per play),
    other enemies (all-enemy damage counts one target).
