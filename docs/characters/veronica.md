# Veronica

What the owner confirmed about this character's cards. The general rules are in [FORMULA.md](../FORMULA.md).

- **Veronica** (owner's screenshots, 2026-11-08, game version 841; E0, Basic 2/10, Unique 8/10, Common 1/10, Attack 906; 3-1 and 7
  unlocked, 5-1 and 5-2 locked): Basic cards x1.04, Unique cards x1.16. The Ballistas (generated cards) take the Signature upgrade.
  - Potential 7 is Card +, measured on the cards: at 906 Attack it's +5% and +1% for each full 40 Attack over 700 (5), so +10%:
    Ballista 186% = (150% + 10%) x 1.16, Piercing and Enhanced 244% = (200% + 10%) x 1.16, Giant 186%, Shelling 128% = (100% + 10%) x 1.16,
    Micro 58% = (40% + 10%) x 1.16. It reads the stats-screen Attack, caps at +15% (from 1,100 Attack), and is 0 below 700.
  - 5-1 (locked) is modeled as Card + on Rapid Fire like the other characters' 5-1, +10% at 500 Attack or higher (**Unconfirmed**: in-game test
    veronica-readings).
  - Reload (User, 2026-11-08): "Increase Damage Amount of Ballista cards by 50%" per stack, multiplicative (no "+"): 3 Reload = x2.5.
    Max 3 stacks, +1 with Bombardment Prep, +1 at E4; set per Ballista play (capped at 5). Ballistas that proc at the end of the turn get
    that turn's Reload (**Unconfirmed**).
  - Piercing Ballista (User, 2026-11-08): its "Piercing Damage" is normal damage, not an Extra Attack, so Extra Damage doesn't raise it
    (**Unconfirmed**). The other five Ballistas are Extra Attacks. Its "+50% Damage Amount" on a Shield target is a per-play switch.
  - "+" rule: Sir Kowalski's, I's and IV's +X% additive on the chosen Ballista (a number per Ballista play, per version), II's "by 30%" and
    Reload multiplicative, V's "decrease by 25%" multiplicative on the plain Ballistas it creates, Floral Scent (E2) +20% additive.
    Enhanced Ballista's +30% is Critical Chance on itself.
  - E6's Mark is the Mark keyword, Veronica's 100% Extra Attack on each Attack Card played with Mark above 0, unraised (as Renoa's).
  - Every Ballista joins the deck with Rapid Fire (3-1 creates a random one); each Firing Preparation version brings its own.
  - Prydwen corrections: the Ballistas other than Piercing read 0% (no Extra Attack phrase); set to their %, hits and Extra Attack kind.
    Wording updated from the game (Pendant of Resolution's "Skill Card of a different Combatant", Sir Kowalski IV's Exhaust, node 7's
    "700 or higher"). Pendant of Resolution IV reads "50%% chance" in game (a typo there), written 50%.
  - Not counted: Shields, draws, Reload gains (set the stacks), card creation, Giant Ballista's other targets (one target).
