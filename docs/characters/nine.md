# Nine

What the owner confirmed about this character's cards. The general rules are in [FORMULA.md](../FORMULA.md).

- **Nine** (owner's screenshots, 2026-11-01; level 1, Attack 138, Defense 50, no Ego, every Potential 0/10 or 0/5, 3-1, 5-1 and 7 locked):
  every in-game % is Prydwen's base at upgrade 0, including every Hew level.
  - Prydwen corrections: Experienced Strike's "Exhaust Pile cards" is "Exhaust cards"; Fatal Strike's Cost is of the card "in hand"; Experienced Strike II's and Fatal Strike IV's "used" is
    "activated"; cards "activate" is "proc" (Hew (Ambush), Counterblade V, Fighting Spirit); Potentials 5-1 and 7 are "or higher" (Prydwen "greater than").
    E4 is "1 Improvisation for each Cost of that card", with no "for 1 turn"; **E6 is 25%** (Prydwen 15%) and can't crit; E5 names Signature (Prydwen Unique).
  - Honed Edge, Experienced Strike's Exhaust card buff and 3-1's Exhaust card buff reach only Nine's cards (User, 2026-11-01). **Unconfirmed in game**; in-game test `nine-readings`.
  - Honed Edge+ (Counterblade IV; on no screenshot): "Increase Defense-Based Damage Amount of Attack Cards of allies by 20% for 1 turn (max 3 stacks)" (User, 2026-11-01).
  - E3's "+150 Damage Amount" has no % in game or on Prydwen: it raises the base, 250% -> 400%, so multipliers raise it too (User, 2026-11-01), and the per-Cost bonus goes to +100%.
    It's `Base %` because `Card +` needs a card with an upgrade and the Ego skill has none; for the Ego skill they give the same number. **Unconfirmed in game**; in-game test `nine-e3`.
  - Hew (Massacre) "Decrease Damage Amount by 10% for each target": -10% with the one target a fight has (User, 2026-11-01), multiplicative under the "+" rule.
  - 3-1's +30% and 5-1's +5% / +5% on Slash are `Card +`, like the other characters' nodes (added before the upgrade). **Unconfirmed for Nine**, who has both locked; in-game test `nine-readings`.
  - Experienced Strike's Exhaust card buff has no stack limit in its text, so plays can stack it. **Unconfirmed**; in-game test `nine-readings`.
  - Exploit Opening (E2): the text is E2's, the keywords Prydwen's (no screenshot of the card); no damage, so switch Vulnerable on for the plays it reaches.
  - Not counted: E6's Fixed damage (waits for the extra-damage model, CLAUDE.md), Counterattacks, Shields and Shield gain, Tenacity Damage. Procs (Fighting Spirit, Hew (Ambush), Counterblade V) are added as plays.
