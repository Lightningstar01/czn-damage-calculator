# Sereniel

What the owner confirmed about this character's cards. The general rules are in [FORMULA.md](../FORMULA.md).

- **Sereniel** (owner's screenshots, 2026-11-08, game version 841; E0, Basic 1/10, Unique 10/10, 3-1 and 7 unlocked, 5-1 locked;
  Attack 923, Critical Chance 59.1%, Critical Damage 168.8%, Instinct Damage 15.0%, Damage over Time 9.5%): Basic cards x1.02,
  Unique cards x1.2 (Homing Laser L too: 72% = 60% x 1.2).
  - Prydwen corrections (in-game numbers at these levels, divided by the upgrade):
    - Death Halo (Ego skill) 500% (Prydwen 400%).
    - Homing Laser 120%, I 180%, II 180%, III 225%, IV 120%, V 180% (Prydwen 100% / 150%).
    - Cobalt Light 140%, I 210%, II–V 140% (Prydwen 120% / 180%).
    - Plasma Missile II's +60% is "base Damage Amount" (Base %). Plasma Missile V's 50% shows unraised at Unique 10, so it has no upgrade.
    - Shining Core V creates a Homing Laser "with their own Signature Epiphanies"; 5-1 and 7 are "20% / 40% or higher" (Prydwen "greater than").
    - Wording of every card, node and Ego level from the game.
  - Potential 7's Instinct Damage is already on the stats screen (15.0% = 10% + 5% at 59.1% crit), so it isn't added again (User, 2026-11-08;
    the same for every character, FORMULA.md, Stats).
  - Afterglow is Sereniel's: +100% additive on her next Attack Card against a Ravaged target, one card per Afterglow, a per-play switch
    (User, 2026-11-08). Its "enemies without Tenacity" half is read through the same Target Ravaged switch: turn Ravaged on for such an
    enemy (**Unconfirmed**, in-game test sereniel-readings).
  - "Proc 1 more time" (Plasma Missile, Cobalt Light V) and Pale Shooting Star's Homing Laser procs are plays added by hand (User, 2026-11-08).
  - "Homing Laser" includes Homing Laser L, for Plasma Missile III's +60%, Cobalt Light IV's hits and E4 (User, 2026-11-08).
  - Plasma Missile V's +30% per Homing Laser moved to hand raises its own end-of-turn hit (User, 2026-11-08).
  - Destroy (Homing Laser I) happens on Ravage or on a Ravaged target: +1 hit while the play's Target Ravaged is on (User, 2026-11-08;
    `ravagedHits`).
  - E6's "+20% Weakness Damage Amount" adds to the Weakness DMG stat (User, 2026-11-08).
  - **Unconfirmed** (in-game test sereniel-readings):
    - Instinct Weakness makes Instinct hits Weakness Attacks, as Selena's Passion Weakness does.
    - E1's +100% on Pale Shooting Star is additive (as Khalipe's E6 on Silver Veil), not part of its shown %.
    - E3's +150% on Death Halo is part of its % (Base %, FORMULA.md, Ego skill bonuses): 650% at E3.
    - E6's On Ravage hit (80% x max Tenacity) is not raised by the card upgrade, and counts as a hit of hers (not an Extra Attack).
  - Not counted: Tenacity damage, Shields, card moves and draws. Hits set by hand: Cobalt Light II (Retain), IV (Homing Lasers in hand)
    and E2's Homing Laser L (+1 hit).
