# Diana

What the owner confirmed about this character's cards. The general rules are in [FORMULA.md](../FORMULA.md).

- **Diana** (owner's screenshots, 2026-11-09, game version 841; E0, Basic 1/10, Common 1/10, Unique 8/10; 3-1 and 7 unlocked, 5-1 and 5-2 locked;
  Attack 774, Critical Chance 47.6%, Extra Damage 11.5% (+23.9%)): Basic cards x1.02, Unique cards x1.16. The generated cards and their
  Extra Attacks take the Signature upgrade: Heart Breaker 93% = 80% x 1.16 and its discard Extra Attack 139% = 120% x 1.16; Cupid Shot
  139% and its per-Bullet Extra Attack 70% = 60% x 1.16; Blooming Love IV 93% = 80% x 1.16.
  - 3-1 adds "Discard 1 random card(s) of this unit" to Disruption Fire (text only). 5-1 (locked) is Card + on Disruption Fire, +10% at
    Critical Chance 20% or higher, like Renoa's 5-1.
  - Potential 7 is on the stats screen (Extra Damage 11.5% = 4% + 37.6 / 5 at Critical Chance 47.6%: the "for every 5%" isn't rounded
    down, like Hugo's 7; in-game test threshold-every-rounding), so nothing is added on top.
  - Resonance (User, 2026-11-09): "On card Discard, 80% Extra Attack, then decrease Resonance by 1". Each activation is one 80% Extra Attack
    by Diana, not raised by card upgrades (the glossary shows 80% at Unique 8), raised by Extra Damage and E4. Set "Resonance activations" on
    the play that set them off: a play that discards cards while the target has Resonance (anyone's), or Pistol of Purification's "activate
    ... 3 / 5 / 10 more times" (max 20 per play).
  - Heart Breaker's "If this card is Discarded, 120% Extra Attack" is set per play ("Heart Breakers Discarded", one hit each) on the play that
    discards them, like Renoa's Dirge Bullet. Cupid Shot's "60% Extra Attack × Bullet card count in hand" is one hit per Bullet card ("Bullet
    cards in hand" on the Cupid Shot play); Blooming Love IV's "80% Extra Attack × that amount" one hit per Bullet card Discarded (its count).
  - Liberated Feelings' "Increase base Damage Amount by 30% for each different Bullet card in hand" raises the card %, as Lucas's Flame Thrower IV
    (User, 2026-11-01): 348% x (1 + 30% x N). Pistol of Purification IV's "+100% base Damage Amount for each Bullet Discarded this turn" is Base %
    (added to the shown 209%).
  - Quietus ("When this card is Discarded, proc this card"): a Quietus card that is Discarded is played; add it to the attack list.
  - E1's "+100% Damage Amount to the Liberated Feelings card" is taken as part of the card's % (Card +, like Hilde's E1; **Unconfirmed**: in-game
    test diana-readings). E2 Flutters is a play at the end of the turn (200% Extra Attack, by 10% per Flutters, multiplicative; max 20, 30
    from E6). E4's +30% and +1% per Flutters are additive on Diana's Extra Attacks (set Flutters on each).
  - E6 (User, 2026-11-09): "allies inflict 200% Extra Attack" at 30 Flutters is one hit by each ally with their own stats; only Diana's is
    counted (a play of its own from E6).
  - Prydwen corrections: the name is "C'mere, Milk & Pong!" (Prydwen "C'mere、Milk & Pong!"); C'mere I creates Heart Shaker (Prydwen Heart
    Breaker); Blooming Love I has "Discard 1"; Blooming Love IV read 0% (set to 80%, Extra Attack, one hit per Bullet); Cupid Shot's Extra
    Attack wasn't read. Wording from the game ("base Damage Amount", "activated", "× that amount").
  - Not counted: the teammates' E6 Extra Attacks, Resonance / Flutters gains and discards (set the numbers per play), heals, draws, cost
    changes, several targets (one target; Pistol V's "all enemies" Resonance is one target's).
