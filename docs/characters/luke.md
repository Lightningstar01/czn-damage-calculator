# Luke

What the owner confirmed about this character's cards. The general rules are in [FORMULA.md](../FORMULA.md).

- **Luke** (screenshots 2026-11-09, game version 841, the same as Fei's that day; **Unconfirmed**, not shown in the screenshots): the owner doesn't have
  Luke yet, so these are the Combatant Archive preview: Lv 1, every Potential node 0, no Ego level, Critical Chance 3%, Critical Damage 125%.
  The card %s match Prydwen's base %s (no upgrade).
  - Prydwen corrections: "Rapid Fire (Luke)" is **Chain Fire** (I–V); "Tracer" is **Tracer Shot**; Finisher Round is **500%** (Prydwen 300%),
    [Unique / Bullet], Unactivatable until 5 Handgun Bullet cards are activated; Dance of the Demon V is **50% × 6** (Prydwen × 5); the Ego skill
    Lethal Design is **450%** with "+25% base Damage Amount for each card in hand" (Prydwen 0%); E3 is "+100% Damage Amount to Lethal Design, and
    ... for each card in hand is changed from +25% to +35%" (Prydwen +50%, 15% to 25%); E5 names the Signature Card Upgrade; E6's card is the
    **High-Velocity Armor Piercing (HVAP) Round**. Its picture is Magnum Shot's (no art of its own on cznmetadecks). Every card, node and Ego
    level now has the in-game wording.
  - **Handgun Bullet** (in game): Magnum Shot, Silver Shot, Uranium Shot, Tracer Shot. Chain Fire (not IV / V) and Stealth Reload create them,
    so they join the deck with those cards; the owner adds one play per bullet created. They're Signature-upgraded cards, like other created
    cards (**Unconfirmed**, in-game test luke-readings).
  - **Weakness Attack lines** (Silver Shot's "Increase Damage Amount by 100%", multiplicative; Dance of the Demon III's "+50% Damage Amount",
    additive) count only on plays that hit a Weakness: the fight's enemy weak to Order (or to all elements), or the play's own switch
    (User, 2026-11-09; FORMULA.md, Weakness).
  - **Mark** (Tracer Shot "1 Mark"): the Mark keyword, the same for every character (User, 2026-11-09): every Attack Card play with Mark (Luke)
    above 0 adds Luke's 100% Extra Attack.
  - **Per-play switches** (User, 2026-11-09): Stealth Reload I's "+30% Critical Chance to those cards" ('From Stealth Reload I') and E2's
    "Decreases Damage Amount of cards created through this effect by 50%" ('Created by E2', multiplicative) on each Handgun Bullet play.
  - Other per-play counters: Dance of the Demon's "Add 1 Hit for each Bullet card (II: Attack Card) activated this turn"; Seize the Opportunity's
    "+X% Damage Amount to the next Bullet card (IV: Attack Card) activated" (III: "for 1 turn"), one switch per version; Seize the Opportunity V
    (an Upgrade) is always on while in the deck; Uranium Shot's "If the target has Shield, increase Damage Amount by 100%" (multiplicative);
    Lethal Design's cards in hand (Base %, as other Ego skill bonuses); E4 / E6 Target Analysis stacks (+10% / +15% each, max 4) on Bullet cards.
  - Chain Fire's Critical Chance (+50%, V +70%; III "Guaranteed Critical" as +100%) counts on that card only; HVAP Round's "Guaranteed Critical"
    too (its wording is Prydwen's: not in the screenshots).
  - 5-1 ("+5% Damage Amount to Luke's Single Shot", +5% more at Critical Chance 20% or higher) and 7 ("+3% Damage Amount to Handgun Bullet"
    at Critical Chance 40% or higher, +2% for every 5% over 40%, +12% max) raise the card's %, like Renoa's 5-1 and 7 (**Unconfirmed**, in-game
    test luke-readings). A Critical Chance exactly on a step counts it (50% = 2 steps).
  - Not counted: Lead (cost), Shields, draws, cost changes; how many bullets Chain Fire's crits, E2 and E6 create (add those plays by hand);
    Finisher Round's Unactivatable condition (play it when it's unlocked).
