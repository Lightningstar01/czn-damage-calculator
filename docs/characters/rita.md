# Rita

What the owner confirmed about this character's cards. The general rules are in [FORMULA.md](../FORMULA.md).

- **Rita** (owner's screenshots, 2026-11-08; level 60, Attack 881, Defense 221, Health 574, Critical Chance 49.7%, Critical Damage 152.4%, Justice Damage 16%, Extra Damage 11.3%, Damage over Time 6.4%, E1; Basic upgrade 10/10, Common 1/10, Unique 8/10; nodes 3-1, 5-1 and 7 unlocked, 5-2 locked):
  every in-game % is Prydwen's base x 1.20 (Basic) or x 1.16 (Unique), except where listed below. `gameVersion` 841 is taken from the other reviews the same day: the screenshots don't show it.
  - Prydwen corrections (wording only, no numbers): the game says "activated" / "proc" where Prydwen says "used" / "activate"; Future Decreed III procs a 3-Cost card, not an Attack Card, from the Draw Pile; Future Decreed V has no "manually"; Hour of Glory counts "each Cost of cards activated this battle"; E5 raises the Signature Card Upgrade (Prydwen: Unique).
  - **Time Acceleration** 492% = (350% + 3-1's +50% + 5-1's +5% + 5% from Attack 500) x 1.20: both nodes raise the card's % (`Card +`), as for the other characters' 5-1.
  - **Chrono Archon II** and **Future Decreed V** show 100% and 300% at Unique 8, so their damage isn't raised by the upgrade. Each play of the Upgrade card stands for one end of turn (Chrono Archon II, with the Chronal Ascension count) or one card whose Cost was increased, used (Future Decreed V).
  - **Chrono Archon II** hits once per Chronal Ascension, each 100%. **Unconfirmed** (the owner isn't sure, 2026-11-08); in-game test `rita-readings`.
  - **Node 7**'s +10% Attack is already in the stats-screen Attack, so nothing is added on top (User, 2026-11-08). It's reference text.
  - **Time Axis Collapse / I**: 20% (multiplicative) for each Cost of cards in hand, set on the play, max 10 (200%). **II**: 30% for each 1-Cost or lower card in hand, no max. **III**: 100% on the next 3 Attack Cards of Rita, switched on per play.
  - **Hour of Glory**: 10% for each Cost of cards activated this battle, max 200%, on Rita's next Attack Card. At E3 it stays per Cost, 20% each, max 300% (User, 2026-11-08).
  - **Fate Distortion** (E2): 30%, plus 10% for each Cost of the Attack Card it raises (User, 2026-11-08: the calculator reads the card's Cost, `perCardCost`). The Cost is the one the card is played at (a Fate Imprint's raised Cost). **Unconfirmed**; in-game test `rita-readings`.
  - **Fate Imprint**: 120% (139% at Unique 8) x the Cost it's played at, set on each play. Chrono Circle (all but III and V) brings it into the deck.
  - **E4 Trace of Memory**: 5% on all of Rita's damage per stack, max 6, set on each play.
  - Not counted: E6's 80% Fixed Damage for each Chronal Ascension used (it waits for the damage-parts model, CLAUDE.md), Chronicle and Chronal Ascension as resources, cost changes, procs (add the procced card as a play), Vulnerable unless set on the play.
