# Heidemarie

What the owner confirmed about this character's cards. The general rules are in [FORMULA.md](../FORMULA.md).

- **Heidemarie** (owner's screenshots, 2026-11-09, game version 841; E2, Basic 1/10, Unique 8/10, Common 1/10, Attack 960; 3-1 and 7 unlocked,
  5-1 locked, so 5-1's switch is on until turned off on Setup > Potentials): Basic cards x1.02 (Sword Flash 102%), Unique and generated
  cards x1.16 (Sword Rain 93%, Effulgent Blade 139%, Thread of Light V 406% / 348%).
  - 7 (in game): "If Heidemarie's Attack is 600 or higher, +5% Passion Attribute Damage Amount, and for every 20 Attack exceeding 600, add an
    additional +2% (additional +10% max)". It's on the stats screen (Passion Damage 15.0% = 5% + 10% at Attack 960), so nothing is added
    on top, like Selena's 7.
  - 5-1: "+5% to Sword Flash, +5% more at Attack 500 or higher", Card + like the other characters' 5-1 (**Unconfirmed** here: locked).
  - E1 (in game): Effulgent Release 812% = (500% + 200%) x 1.16, so E1's +200% is part of the card % (Card +). Its per-Blade bonus is
    "+120% base Damage Amount" in game (Prydwen: "+100% Damage Amount"): Base %. Below E1 the calculator uses "+100% base"
    (**Unconfirmed**, in-game test `heidemarie-readings`).
  - Buffs "to Effulgent Blade" (3-1's +30%, Effulgent Expansion's +50%, I's +70%, II's +200%, V's +50%) raise all the Blade's damage:
    the Blade played, its "When Discarded, 139% Extra Attack" and Sword Rain III's procs (User, 2026-11-09). Those two hits carry the
    Effulgent Blade and Linked tags, so Thread of Light III's +80% to Linked cards reaches them too (**Unconfirmed**). The "for 1 turn"
    buffs are on/off per play: two Sword Flash or Expansion plays in a turn count once (**Unconfirmed**).
  - Discarded Blades: an "Effulgent Blades discarded" number on any play, by anyone while Heidemarie is in the team, adds that many
    139% Extra Attacks, computed with her build (as Renoa's Dirge Bullets).
  - Sword Rain III: "Proc 5 Effulgent Blade in Graveyard against the target as an Extra Attack" = 5 Extra Attacks of the Blade's 139%.
    Sword Rain II: add 1 hit on the play per Blade in hand.
  - Thread of Light II's "3x the Damage" triples the whole hit, a Final multiplier (User, 2026-11-09). Thread of Light V's discard
    damage (348%) is a play of its own, raised by the upgrade (measured).
  - E2 Crimson Blade: a "Crimson Blade activations" number per play, each a 120% Extra Attack by Heidemarie, not raised by card upgrades
    (User, 2026-11-09). One activation per Linked card used or Discarded while Crimson Blade lasts (5 per turn, max 8). E6's +40% per
    activation counts with "Crimson Blade activations so far" (max 12 = +480%).
  - "+" rule: the Ego skill's per-card bonus, Thread of Light's per-Linked bonus, Sword Rain V's per-Blade bonus and E4's Effulgent Chain
    are additive; E3's +200% to Elysium of Blades is Base % (FORMULA.md, Ego skill bonuses).
  - Prydwen corrections: Effulgent Compression is unplayable (Prydwen: 0 Cost); Effulgent Expansion I is [Linked / Quietus] and II
    [Quietus] (Prydwen: none); the Blade's discard hit is an Extra Attack (Prydwen: "Extra Damage"); "before activation" on Sword
    Rain II and Thread of Light; Sword Rain III's "Proc"; Thread of Light V's "When used" (Prydwen: "When used manually"); 5-1 and 7
    "or higher"; E5 [Signature Card Upgrade]. Not counted: Shields, draws, E6's All Attribute Blades, Thread of Light IV's procs (add the
    procced cards as plays).
