# Lucas

What the owner confirmed about this character's cards. The general rules are in [FORMULA.md](../FORMULA.md).

- **Lucas** (owner's screenshots, 2026-11-01; Ego 6, Basic and Unique cards x1.12 (level 0, E3 and E5 +6: badges 6/16); 3-1, 5-1 and 7 locked,
  so they're off on Setup > Potentials until unlocked): Machine Gun 112%, Launcher Bullets 157%, Flame Thrower 202%, Flashbang 134%, Full Burst 300%.
  - Prydwen corrections:
    - R.P.G-7 is "50% Fixed Damage" in game at E1 (Prydwen: "40 Fixed Damage"): 40% + E1's 10%, unraised.
    - Flame Thrower IV is "Increase base Damage Amount by 50% for each Exhausted card" (Prydwen: "Increase Damage Amount").
    - 5-1 and 7 are "160% / 240% or higher" (Prydwen: "greater than"). S.S.S IV is "+120% Damage Amount" (Prydwen: "+120% Damage").
    - E6 is "Decrease Cost of the first Launcher Bullet card activated each turn by 1". E3 and E5 name the Signature Card Upgrade (Prydwen: Unique).
  - R.P.G-7 "When Bullet cards are Exhausted" (User, 2026-11-01): a hit by itself per Bullet card with Exhaust played, teammates' too (Renoa's Dirge Bullet), one per Bullet Flame Thrower IV exhausts (Bullets Exhausted on
    that play) and one per Bullet Ephemeral exhausts at the end of the turn (Bullets exhausted by Ephemeral, on any play). Machine Gun with 3-1 has Bullet but not Exhaust, so it
    doesn't set it off. S.S.S V likewise reaches any Bullet card. **Unconfirmed:** that Ephemeral's exhaust counts in game (in-game test lucas-readings).
  - Flame Thrower III "On Critical Hit" (User, 2026-11-01): expected damage x the card's crit chance; a crit counts it in full, a non-crit doesn't. A hit on all enemies
    crits on all of them or none, so it's one roll. It's a normal hit, raised like the card (168% = 150% x 1.12), that can crit too (**Unconfirmed:** in-game test lucas-readings).
    **Unconfirmed:** with Hits raised on the play, it's still one roll, on the first hit (in-game test lucas-readings).
  - Flame Thrower IV (User, 2026-11-01): raises the card %, 302% x (1 + 50% x 2) = 604% with 2 Bullets.
  - Flashbang II and V reach only Lucas's hits (User, 2026-11-01). V reads Target has Shield; II has its own switch, Flashbang II on a Shield target, since it also needs II played that turn.
  - Flashbang IV's Mark (User, 2026-11-01): Lucas's own 100% Extra Attack on Attack plays with Mark (Lucas) set, a counter apart from Selena's Mark, unraised, as hers is.
    **Unconfirmed:** the keyword's wording on this card isn't in the screenshots, and neither is whether it's Lucas's hit or the Mark Selena's buffs reach (in-game test lucas-readings).
  - Agony, Impair and Weaken are text only for now (User, 2026-11-01); Agony waits for the DoT work.
  - The S.S.S / I / II Bullet buffs are additive per the "+" rule, on the plays set per turn. **Unconfirmed:** that two of them in a turn stack; the count is left open
    (in-game test lucas-readings).
  - E2's +20% is additive: it isn't in the shown % (Launcher Bullets show 157% = 140% x 1.12 at E6). It reaches every Bullet card, Machine Gun too with 3-1.
    **Unconfirmed:** Machine Gun's share of E2 and the S.S.S buffs with 3-1 (in-game test lucas-readings).
  - 5-1's and 7's +5% (+5% more at Critical Damage 160% / 240%, stats screen) add before the upgrade (`Card +`), following the owner's rule for Potential nodes. Both are locked, so
    the cards haven't been seen with them (**Unconfirmed:** in-game test lucas-readings).
  - S.S.S V's and R.P.G-7's Fixed %s are unraised at x1.12 (50%), as on Orlea's Annoying.
  - E4: Full Burst 300% = 250% + 50%, Draw 3 (an Ego skill has no upgrade).
  - Not counted: the Launcher Bullets' 70% to another enemy (fights have one target for now), Agony, Impair, Weaken, Shields, draws, cost changes.
