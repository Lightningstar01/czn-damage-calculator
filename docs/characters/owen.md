# Owen

What the owner confirmed about this character's cards. The general rules are in [FORMULA.md](../FORMULA.md).

- **Owen** (owner's screenshots, 2026-11-01; Ego 4, Basic and Unique cards x1.06 (level 0, E3 +3: badges 3/13); 3-1, 5-1 and 7 locked,
  so they're off on Setup > Potentials until unlocked): Downward Cut 106%, Wind Slash 297%, Break Armor 148%, Wind Riding 159%.
  - Prydwen corrections:
    - Wind Charge II reaches "the next Attack Card activated" (any ally's), not "of this unit". V stays Owen's own.
    - Wind Riding V is "If Wind Charge is activated this turn, gain additional 220% Shield" (Prydwen: activate this card 1 more time).
    - Wind Slash III says "max 1 time". Wind Riding II and Wind Slash IV are shown unraised at 120% and 280%.
  - Wind Charge II / V "Decrease Damage Amount": only the next Attack Card, the one that gets the added hits, not the whole turn (User, 2026-11-01).
    Switched on per play; the added hits are that play's hit count. **Unconfirmed:** whether two of them before the same card stack
    (one switch for now; in-game test owen-readings).
  - Wind Charge III "30% chance to add 1 Hit(s)": 30% of one more hit on each of Owen's own Attack plays, as expected value (User, 2026-11-01).
    It's a hit after the card's own, so first-hit buffs don't reach it.
  - Break Armor I "Decrease Fortitude by 4 for 1 turn": +20% Damage taken per Fortitude decreased, so +80% (User, 2026-11-01). Additive by the
    "+" rule, and on the plays after it, like Adelheid's "+100% Damage Amount taken by the target" (**Unconfirmed:** in-game test owen-readings).
  - Break Armor III / IV's "increase Damage Amount by 30%" are multiplicative switches on their own plays (Target in a Vulnerable state, Target
    has Shield). The first doesn't read the play's Vulnerable switch: set both.
  - Potential 7's +4% Attack and Defense (+4% more at Health 700) counts on the stats screen, so nothing is added on top (User, 2026-11-01).
  - 3-1's +30% and 5-1's +5% (+5% more at Health 600) on Downward Cut add before the upgrade (`Card +`), following the owner's rule for Potential nodes. Both
    are locked, so the card hasn't been seen with them (**Unconfirmed:** in-game test owen-readings).
  - E1 is part of the shown %, on the Shield too: Gale Strike 371% = (300% + 50%) x 1.06, 191% = (130% + 50%) x 1.06. E4: Raging Storm 300% = 250% + 50%
    (an Ego skill has no upgrade).
  - Effect hits are normal damage, unraised by card upgrades and skipped by Attack Card buffs, as on Selena and Yuki: Wind Riding II's 120% on each Wind Charge
    (adds itself), Wind Slash IV's 280% at the end of the turn (one play per turn), E2's 80% (one play per Wind Charge moved to hand). The game's unraised
    numbers show this for the first two. **Unconfirmed** for E2, and whether Attack Card buffs reach them (in-game test owen-readings).
  - E6 "Each time the Wind Charge card is activated, +20% Damage Amount": additive, one stack per Wind Charge that turn (**Unconfirmed:** whether it
    stacks; the owner doesn't have E6 yet; in-game test owen-readings).
  - Not counted: Tenacity damage, Shields, Damage Reduction, moved cards, Wind Charge IV's proc of a Skill Card (no Skill Card of Owen's deals damage).
