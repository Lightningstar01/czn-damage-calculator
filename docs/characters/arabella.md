# Arabella

What the owner confirmed about this character's cards. The general rules are in [FORMULA.md](../FORMULA.md).

- **Arabella** (owner's screenshots, 2026-11-09; E0 (all six levels locked), Basic 1/10, Common 1/10, Unique 9/10; 3-1 and 7 unlocked, 5-1 and 5-2 locked;
  Attack 870, Instinct Damage 31.0% (15.0% + 16.0%), Damage over Time 32.7%, Extra Damage 14.7%, Critical Chance 46.4%, Critical Damage 183.8%):
  Basic cards x1.02 (Diagonal Slash 61%, Crimson Domain 102% Shield), the others x1.18 (Scream For Me 47%, its epiphanies 71%, Hollow Verdict III 425%,
  Sadistic Restraint III 283%, The Art of Unmaking 236%). Every in-game % is Prydwen x the upgrade; the keyword's 40% (Fracture) isn't raised.
  - Fracture (User, 2026-11-09; **Unconfirmed**, in-game test fracture-tick): "At the end of the turn, 40% DoT for each Fracture" is one hit of
    40% x the stacks (one crit roll), normal ATK damage (it doesn't say Fixed), so card buffs, Vulnerable, Instinct and the DoT stat raise it. Max 30 stacks.
  - Activations (User, 2026-11-09): a Fracture play comes with the Ego skill, so it's always in the deck. Add one per activation (end of turn,
    Hollow Verdict, each of Sadistic Restraint's "2 times") and type the stacks before it halves. Nothing tracks stacks: add what cards inflict and halve after each activation.
  - Fracture Amplification: "Increase Damage Amount of Fracture inflicted by Arabella by 20%" is multiplicative (no "+"), set per play
    ("Fracture Amplification on target", which The Art of Unmaking's 2x and E6's Critical Damage also read). Its added Fracture on activation isn't counted.
  - "+X% Damage Amount to this unit for 1 turn" (Observer's Pleasure / I / II / IV) reaches all her damage, Fracture ticks included (User, 2026-11-09;
    **Unconfirmed**, in-game test arabella-readings). Additive, set per play. "Increase Damage Amount of Attack Cards of this unit by 50%" (Scream For Me II,
    Observer's Pleasure III) is multiplicative on Attack Cards only, so not on Fracture.
    Observer's Pleasure / I count the enemies present (the stepper stops at 5; how many enemies a fight can have isn't known).
  - The Art of Unmaking (User, 2026-11-09): "2x base Damage Amount" with Fracture Amplification doubles the card's % (Card % +100%: 472% at Unique 9),
    and "by 15% for each type of harmful effect" multiplies on top (max 8 types = 120%; E1: 20%, max 9 types = 180%).
    E1's "+50% Damage Amount" is taken as part of the card's % (Card +), by the owner's rule for Ego "+X%" (Chizuru's E1, measured); E1 is locked, so
    **Unconfirmed** (in-game test arabella-readings).
  - Sadistic Restraint's added hits are set on the play: / and I, 1 + the harmful effect types (max 3 more); IV, 5 with "Efficiency: Add 1 Hit"; V, 1 + the
    Fractures activated this turn (max 5 more).
  - 5-1 (locked) is Card + on Diagonal Slash, +5% more at Attack 500 or higher, like Chizuru's 5-1 (**Unconfirmed**, arabella-readings). 7 is on the stats screen
    (at 870 Attack it gives 5% + the 10% max = 15%, inside the screen's Instinct Damage +16.0%; see FORMULA.md, Stats), so nothing is added on top.
  - E3 "+200% Damage Amount" to Pathetic End is added to the Ego skill's 600% (Base %), as Chizuru's E3. E4 Deconstruction Analysis "by 5%" per stack
    is multiplicative on Fracture (max 6, 10 from E6), set per play. E6's +50% Critical Damage counts on Fracture plays with Fracture Amplification on target.
  - Prydwen corrections: wording throughout (Scream For Me II "unity"; Sadistic Restraint IV "Efficiency: Add 1 Hit", Prydwen "If Cost of this card is
    decreased, add 1 Hit"; Hollow Verdict V "remove all Fracture"; E2 / E3 / E4 / E6 wording). No numbers differ.
  - Not counted: Pathetic End's Fixed damage (30% / 50% of damage taken, capped per turn; it waits for the extra-damage model), Fracture a card inflicts
    (add it to the count), Hollow Verdict V's moved Fracture, Observer's Pleasure IV's transferred Fracture, Agony and Scorched (Scream For Me V,
    Hollow Verdict IV), E2's Fracture, Shields, draws, other enemies (all-enemy damage counts one target).
